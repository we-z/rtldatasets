# Digital delivery and refund policy

Effective August 1, 2026

After Stripe confirms payment, the purchase browser receives immediate download
access for 12 hours. Download access is revalidated against the current payment,
refund, and dispute state.

If the checkout return is interrupted, revisiting this site in the same browser
within seven days automatically attempts to restore a fresh 12-hour access
window. This recovery requires the browser's essential checkout data to remain
available. Download and save the archive from the confirmation page.

If automated recovery does not complete while the payment remains active,
contact `root@puul.ai` from the checkout email address. We will verify the
purchase and restore access.

Because this is an immediately delivered digital product, sales are final after
access is issued, except where required by law. We will review duplicate
charges, failure to deliver, corrupted archives, or a product that is materially
different from the published description. Contact us within seven days of
purchase with the checkout email address and a description of the issue.

A refund, reversal, or payment dispute disables future download access. This
policy does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be waived.
