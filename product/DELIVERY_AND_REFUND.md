# Digital delivery and refund policy

Effective August 1, 2026

After Stripe confirms payment, the purchase browser receives download access
for 12 hours and Cloudflare Email Service sends a private redemption link to the
checkout email address. The emailed link can restore browser access for 30 days
after purchase. Download access is revalidated against the current payment,
refund, and dispute state.

If automated delivery fails or the redemption link expires while the payment
remains active, contact `root@puul.ai` from the checkout email address. We will
verify the purchase and restore access.

Because this is an immediately delivered digital product, sales are final after
access is issued, except where required by law. We will review duplicate
charges, failure to deliver, corrupted archives, or a product that is materially
different from the published description. Contact us within seven days of
purchase with the checkout email address and a description of the issue.

A refund, reversal, or payment dispute disables future download access. This
policy does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be waived.
