Privacy notice

Effective August 1, 2026

RTL Datasets uses purchase information only to process payment, deliver the Sample, prevent fraud and duplicate fulfillment, provide support, maintain transaction records, and respond to refunds or disputes.

Information processed

  • Stripe processes card and billing information. RTL Datasets does not receive or store full card numbers.
  • RTL Datasets stores the checkout email address, Stripe session/payment identifiers, purchased artifact version and checksum, delivery status, and download timestamps.
  • Essential signed cookies hold checkout state for up to 30 minutes and private download access for up to 12 hours. They are HTTP-only and are not used for advertising.
  • Cloudflare provides site hosting, private object storage, database storage, security, and transactional email delivery, and may process IP address, device, request, and security-log data while providing those services.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared with Stripe and Cloudflare only as needed to operate payment and fulfillment, or when legally required. Transaction and delivery records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for accounting, security, support, and dispute handling.

RTL Datasets does not use the purchase flow for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking. Stripe and Cloudflare process information under their own service and privacy terms.

Requests

Subject to applicable law and records we must retain, a purchaser may request access to, correction of, or deletion of purchase information by contacting us from the checkout email address. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Contact

For privacy questions or a request concerning your purchase information, email root@puul.ai from the checkout email address.
